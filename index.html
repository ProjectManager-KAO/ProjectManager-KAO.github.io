AboutОбо мне

Requirements. Delivery. Acceptance.Требования. Поставка. Приёмка.

I am a project and delivery manager with 13 years of professional experience: 2.5+ years delivering government IT systems through external vendors and 6.5 years managing international B2G supply contracts, from tender through production control at paper mills, printing works and mints to final delivery.

I own scope, schedule, budget, risk and change control end to end, from requirements to acceptance, as the single point of contact between client executives and delivery teams.

I delivered three Ministry of Finance IT products with zero revision cycles, saved $120k in six months by replacing an external vendor with an in-house team, and managed €70M+ in export contracts across 11 countries with zero missed deadlines.

I work with Agile (Scrum) and Waterfall, and negotiate in English with central bank and ministry leadership.

Я руководитель проектов и поставки с 13-летним профессиональным опытом: 2,5+ года — реализация государственных IT-систем через внешних подрядчиков, 6,5 лет — управление международными B2G-контрактами на поставку продукции, от тендера до контроля производства на бумажных и печатных фабриках, монетных дворах и отгрузки.
